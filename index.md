## Ongoing research projects



<img style="float: left;" src="images/Summary_projects.png" alt="" width="65%"/>  
Our research projects focus on identifying the regulating factors of inter-plant variability and exploring its functional role. To that end, we have 3 ongoing interconnected projects. Find an overview of the projects in [this seminar](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=31X57xWZ0iE). 

<br>

### Regulation of inter-plant variability for genes involved in nitrate nutrition

We found that one of the main transporters of nitrate, *NRT2.1*, has a high level of inter-plant gene expression variability. This project aims at understanding how the level of *NRT2.1* gene expression inter-plant variability is controlled, when differences are established between plants and what are the consequences of this variability.   
Find a more detailed description of the project [here](files/NRT2.md)

<br>

### The role of chromatin in controlling the level of inter-plant variability

Based on previous observation in [Cortijo et al., 2019](https://link.springer.com/article/10.15252/msb.20188591), this project aims at exploring the role of chromatin in regulating the level of inter-plant gene expression variability.  
Find a more detailed description of the project [here](files/Chromatin.md)

<br>

### Influence of the environment on the level of inter-plant variability

While inter-plant variability is now recognized as widespread, observed across phenotypes such as plant size, flowering time, gene expression, and the abundance of proteins and metabolites, its response to environmental changes remains largely unexplored. This project aims at characterizing and understand the response of inter-plant variability to changes in the environment.  
Find a more detailed description of the project [here](files/Environment.md)

## Tools

I developed 2 Shiny web-applications to explore results from papers:  

- [Aranoisy](https://jlgroup.shinyapps.io/AraNoisy/) allows you to follow the level of inter-plant variability in gene expression for your gene of interest in *Arabidopsis thaliana* throughout a day/night cycle.  

- [Variability Network](https://jlgroup.shinyapps.io/VariabilityNetwork/) examines gene expression networks inferred using inter-plant variability in gene expression.
